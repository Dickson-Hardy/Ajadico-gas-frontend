1. Executive Summary & Operational Goals

The Ajadico Filling Station Management System is a unified, cloud-first management platform engineered to automate forecourt fuel operations, eliminate cash and volume shrinkage, and establish strict branch profitability across five retail stations (Lekki Road, Ikeja, Victoria Island, Surulere, and Ajah Terminal).

Initially supporting Premium Motor Spirit (PMS / Petrol) and Automotive Gas Oil (AGO / Diesel), the software operates in parallel with existing physical record books during rollout, ensuring operational continuity while establishing indisputable digital accountability.

Primary Business Mandate: Replace delayed, error-prone end-of-month accounting with instant, shift-by-shift reconciliation where every litre dispensed through pump nozzles is verified against physical bank receipts, POS terminals, and physical note counts.

4. Operational Daily Cycle & Mathematical Formulas

A. Expected Shift Sales Value Formula (§2.10)

The system computes the monetary value of fuel dispensed based on mechanical meter dials:

Litres Sold = Closing Meter Reading − Opening Meter Reading
Nozzle Sales Value = Litres Sold × Active Unit Price
Total Expected Shift Sales = ∑ (Nozzle Sales Value across all assigned nozzles)

Mid-Shift Price Change Periodization (§2.9): When Senior authorizes a price adjustment during an active shift, the system captures an instant meter snapshot ($M_{switch}$), splitting sales mathematically:

Expected Sales = ((M_switch − M_open) × Price_Old) + ((M_close − M_switch) × Price_New)

B. Attendant Shift Remittance & Variance Attribution (§4.6, §4.7)

Declared funds across four payment routes plus verified credit must equal the expected sales value:

Money Declared = Cash + POS Card Swipes + Station POS Transfers + Company Bank Transfers
Attendant Variance = (Money Declared + Verified Credit Sales) − Total Expected Shift Sales
Strict Accounting Rule: Software figures are never altered to artificially force a balance. Shortfalls ($Variance < 0$) are locked directly into the attendant’s salary deduction register. Excesses ($Variance > 0$) are preserved as company payable to staff.

C. The Physical Cash Drawer Equation (§5.3)

Physical cash in the cashier's safe is audited at close of business using denomination counting:

Expected Closing Cash = Opening Cash + Cash Receipts − Physical Cash Expenses − Handed Over for Deposit
Counted Cash = ∑ (Denomination Value × Count of Notes for ₦1000, ₦500, ₦200, ₦100, ₦50, ₦20, ₦10)
Cash Variance = Counted Cash − Expected Closing Cash
Separation of Cash vs Bank (§5.2): Operating expenses paid via bank transfer are strictly excluded from cash drawer deductions. Cash handed over to couriers for commercial bank deposit remains held in an [Awaiting bank] state until Senior verifies the bank credit alert.

5. Anti-Fraud & Revenue Leakage Prevention Engine

The software enforces five strict systemic controls to eliminate the primary sources of fuel retail shrinkage:

Shrinkage Threat Traditional Forecourt Vulnerability Ajadico Systemic Countermeasure
Fake Bank Alerts & Transfer Slips Customers show fake debit SMS alerts on their phone. Attendants accept them and leave station with a cash shortfall. Uploading a payment screenshot never confirms funds. Two-tier verification: Cashier matches terminal printout; Senior matches corporate bank alert before funds are confirmed.
Clerical Shortage Massaging Attendants or cashiers alter closing meter figures or claim higher opening dials to make accounts balance on paper. Previous shift closing readings automatically lock as the next shift's opening baseline. Overrides require manager PIN with permanent historical audit trail (§2.5).
Informal Forecourt Credit Attendants dispense fuel to associates or transport operators on informal promises to pay later. Credit sales can only be logged against accounts pre-authorized by Senior. Captures mandatory vehicle registration, driver name, litres, and signed requisition upload.
Tanker Discharge Shrinkage Delivery drivers claim a full 33,000L discharge while withholding volume in compartments or suffering transit loss. Mandatory Before & After physical tank dip measurements compute exact received litres. The variance against stated waybill litres is automatically logged.
False Shortages from Calibration Pump meter calibration tests or generator diesel withdrawals are counted as missing sales. Screen 07 logs 20L check measure can pour-backs and internal generator fuel, updating calculated tank stock without generating cash shortage.

6. Offline-First Forecourt Resilience Strategy

Forecourt operations in Nigeria cannot depend on 100% internet reliability. The mobile application incorporates an offline-first architecture:

  • Local Encrypted SQLite Storage: Shift assignments, nozzle baselines, and active prices are cached locally on the tablet.
  • Offline PIN Authentication: Attendant PIN hashes are verified locally, allowing instant login even when the cellular network is down.
  • Local Image Staging: Photos of POS receipts and driver requisitions are compressed and staged on local flash storage.
  • Idempotent Background Synchronization: When Wi-Fi/cellular connectivity restores, the background sync queue flushes records using immutable UUIDs, preventing duplicate submissions if a connection drops mid-upload (§9.6).

9. BRD Section 7: Key Open Business Decisions

Section 7 of BRD v3 identified four operational questions requiring confirmation before production deployment:

1. Interlocked Tanks Protocol (Ikeja & Surulere):
Implemented Solution: When switching from Tank 1 to Tank 2 mid-shift, the app prompts for the switch time, meter readings on the connected nozzles, and physical dips on both tanks. Litres dispensed before the switch timestamp are deducted from Tank 1; subsequent litres are deducted from Tank 2.
2. Delivery Measurement During Active Dispensing:
Implemented Solution: When receiving fuel, if dispensing continues on that tank, the delivery form logs the nozzle opening meters at tanker connection and closing meters at tanker disconnect: Actual Received = (Dip After − Dip Before) + Litres Dispensed During Discharge.
3. Informal Credit & Salary Deduction Thresholds:
Implemented Solution: Shift shortages under ₦5,000 are flagged for routine payroll deduction; shortages exceeding ₦5,000 trigger an immediate alert to Senior for formal authorization or waiver.
4. Month-End Executive Profit Layout:
Implemented Solution: Fuel purchases are treated as current inventory assets. COGS is recognized on nozzle sales using weighted average cost, producing accurate Gross Margin and Net Operating Profit after branch expenses.
